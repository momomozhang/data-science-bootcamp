## Data analysis & Visualization

# The "5 W's + H" Method
- What: What patterns exist in the data?
- When: How do things change over time?
- Where: How do different regions/countries compare?
- Who: Which entities perform best/worst?
- Why: What might explain the patterns I see?
- How: How are different variables related?

# "Zoom level" approach
- Marco level: big picture
    - Are there clear energy trends for DE / FR in 1993-2003?
- Meso level: group comparisons
    - what role did differnt fossil fules/coal/oil/gas play in each country?
- Micro level: detailed analysis
    - which specific years showed the biggest changes and why?
    - how did energy efficiency change year by year?

# "So what?"
provide an observation, then ask:
- so what? what does this mean? what it suggests? Are there further questions this lead to?
- why should anyone care?
- what decisions could this inform?

# The "Stakeholder perspective" method
ask: "If I were X, what would I want to know?"

- Policy maker:
    - which policies work?
- Investor:
    - Where are the growth opportunities?
    - Which markets are attractive?
- Citizen:
    - How does this affect energy costs?

# The "Data-Driven Discovery" Process
- Phase 1: Explore First
    - Look at distributions (what's normal vs unusual?)
    - Sort by extremes (highest/lowest values)
    - Check correlations (what moves together?)
    - Look for outliers (what's different and why?)
- Phase 2: Pattern-Based Questions
    - "I noticed X is increasing while Y is decreasing - why?"
    - "Country Z is an outlier - what makes it different?"
    - "There's a sharp change in year Y - what happened?"
